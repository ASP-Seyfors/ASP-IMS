External Marketplace Manager

ℹ️ How to Use the External Marketplace Manager (Click to Expand / Collapse) Quick Guide
1. Choose a Platform: Select RevMed, DotMed, or Vetcove below to manage listings for that storefront.
2. Work Through the 4 Action Queues:
  • 🆕 Not Listed (In-Stock): Available warehouse items not yet uploaded to the selected site. Use the displayed REF, GTIN, Price, Available Qty, and Lot/Exp dates to list the item on the external site, then click + Mark Listed.
  • 📉 Qty Changed / Sold: Listed items whose available warehouse stock changed (from direct orders, customer reservations, or new receiving). Update the quantity on the site (or remove if SOLD OUT), then click ✓ Confirm Updated.
  • 💲 Price Changed: Listed items whose marketplace price was updated in the Database Editor. Adjust the price on the site, then click ✓ Confirm Updated.
  • 📋 Active on Site: All items currently marked as live on the site. Click Unlist if an item is removed from the marketplace.
3. Batch Sync & Worksheets: Use ✓ Mark All Shown as Synced after completing a full queue, or click Print Action Worksheet (PDF) at the bottom for a paper checklist.
